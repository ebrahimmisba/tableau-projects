# tableau-projects on Retail Business Performance Analysis: Sales, Customer, and Product Insights.

The project analyzes retail business performance from three main perspectives by transforming raw retail transaction data into meaningfull business insights:

1. Sales Performance: Understanding sales, profit, order volume, and their trends over time.
2. Customer Analysis: Understanding customer distribution, customer purchasing behavior, and top-performing customers.
3. Product Analysis: Evaluating product categories and subcategories to identify which products contribute most to sales and profit.

# the project workbook includes two dashboards and some kpi sheets

## dashboard 1: customers dashboard

This dashboard focuses on understanding customer behavior and identifying valuable customers.

# sales dashboard

This dashboard focuses on monitoring overall business performance.

# The overall objective is:

To evaluate retail business performance by analyzing sales trends, profitability, customer purchasing behavior, and product performance to identify opportunities for improving business performance.
